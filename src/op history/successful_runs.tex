\section{Successful runs}
\label{sec: successful runs}

A number of successful runs were performed on the FLiNaK purifer, each with some
technical problems. But the final result was at least partially purified
salt. \par

% TODO: Get exact dates from Kyle
Starting in [Insert Date], the operation of the FLiNaK purifier began.
Following some setbacks, the purifier was successfully operated and
FLiNaK was purified. Unknowingly at the time, the \ch{HF} was not being
introduced to the system, so the purified salt was only sparged with
hydrogen gas and argon. Nonetheless, this resulted in significant reductions
in metallic impurities. Naturally, it was noted that the oxygen content was
not reduced, using combustion analysis. \par

The purification progress was monitored using a residual gas analyzer
(RGA), which
was attached to the off-gas from the purifier. See
\Cref{fig: purifier rga}. The concentration of \ch{H2},
\ch{HF}, and \ch{H2O} were monitored in-situ. It was observed that \ch{H2O}
content decreased as the purification progressed. Without \ch{HF}, this
may have been largely due to free oxygen reduction by hydrogen, rather than
hydrofluorination.
\par

Following the purification and transfer (which was partially facilitated with
heating from a blowtorch), the transfer tank was detached from the purifier and
placed in the argon glovebox.
