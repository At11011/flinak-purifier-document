\section{Maintenance Schedule}
Several pieces of equipment in the FLiNaK purifier require regular maintenance.
This section outlines the regular maintenance that must be performed
on a regular basis to ensure optimum operation.

\subsection{SRS RGA100}

The RGA is a sensitive and complex instrument that requires
maintenance and care to ensure optimum performance, especially in
a corrosive environment. Avoid degas unless bakeout is not
feasible.

\subsection{GFC17 Flow Controller}

The GFC17 Flow Controllers are used to control the flow of
\ch{H2}, \ch{Ar}, and \ch{HF}. They do not require significant maintenance,
aside from periodic filter cleaning.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \begin{tabular}{llll}

\subsection{NI USB-6353}

The National Instruments USB-6353 DAC (Digital-to-analog converter)
used to communicate with
process control equipment used in the purifier. It requires
auto-calibration every two years, or when the ambient temperature
changes.
