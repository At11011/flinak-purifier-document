\section{Reaction Chamber}

This section describes the reaction chamber of the FLiNaK purifier.
The reaction chamber is where the FLiNaK purification takes place.
It is constructed out of Hastelloy C-276, which has excellent
corrosion resistance properties. \cite{c276-resistance}
It consists of a CF flanged lid with 5 \SI{0.5}{in} tubes,
% TODO: Confirm dimensions here
1 \SI{0.75}{in} tube, and a \SI{1}{in} tube topped with a \SI{2.75}{in}
OD CF flange.
\par

The purposes of each of the ports is as follows:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \SI{0.5}{in} gas bubbler dip tube port
  \item \SI{0.5}{in} scrubber line port
  \item \SI{0.5}{in} pressure gauge port (unused in current design, capped)
  \item \SI{0.5}{in} vacuum port (originally thermocouple well)
  \item \SI{0.5}{in} burst disk port
  \item \SI{0.75}{in} salt transfer dip tube port
  \item \SI{1}{in} salt introduction port
\end{enumerate}

Inside of the reaction chamber is a nickel 201 liner. This liner is
designed to hold molten salt during purification. Ni-201 is chosen due to its
excellent corrosion resistance, as described in \Cref{sec: ni alloys}.
Because it is a liner, there is little need for it to be made of a
material with greater structural integrity, as secondary containment
is already present in the form of the C276 reaction chamber. \par
