\section{RGA}

An RGA system is used to measure the concentration of \ch{HF}, \ch{H2},
\ch{H2O}, and other product gases. It is constructed using a 4-way
CF-flanged cross, a LeyBold Turbopump, a KJL Ion Gauge, a
sapphire-sealed leak valve and an SRS RGA100. The system is shown in
\Cref{fig: rga}. The scrubber line is connected with a tee to the
inlet of the leak valve, allowing the scrubber gas to be sampled. \par
